    Download group data (CSV)

    The CSV uses the same 31 columns as the CMU dataset, so the notebook can test the models on your group's typing.

    Model settings

    Lower = stricter. Threshold = average of your own leave-one-out distances + this many standard deviations.

    How it decides

    1 · Capture

    11 keys give 31 timings in seconds: 11 hold times (H), 10 press-to-press gaps (DD) and 10 release-to-press gaps (UD).

    2 · Scale

    Each timing is standardised (z-score) so one slow key can't dominate the distances KNN and PCA use.

    3 · Compare

    Scaled Manhattan distance to your stored profile, a KNN vote among every stored attempt, and a logistic regression probability.

    4 · Decide

    Distance below your threshold means access granted. Moving the threshold trades false rejects (FRR) for false accepts (FAR).
